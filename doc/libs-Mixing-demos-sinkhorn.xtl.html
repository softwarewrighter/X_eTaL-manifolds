programlibs/Mixing/demos/sinkhorn.xtl

Mixing, a small demo: a free positive matrix made doubly stochastic by Sinkhorn's iteration (each row divided by its sum, then each column by its sum, repeated), its row and column sum errors by sweep (hc:s_umErrors), and what the result does to four streams: their mean kept, their differences shrunk, its gain 1. Sinkhorn's iteration is matrix scaling, general mathematics: it is asked of X_eTaL-libraries (docs/xetal-libraries-asks.md, GL3) and written out here meanwhile, as u:s_weep, not made a Mixing function.
