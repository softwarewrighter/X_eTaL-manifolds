programdemos/mhc-explorer/mhc-explorer.xtl

mHC explorer, part 1: the matrices. When a network carries several streams of numbers side by side and mixes them at every layer, what may the mixing be allowed to do? From one residual stream, x + F(x), to n streams mixed by an n by n matrix (hyper-connections, Zhu et al. 2025; mHC, Xie et al. 2025, its equation 3); the doubly stochastic matrices of size 3, nine entries and four free; the path from the identity to uniform mixing; Sinkhorn's iteration from a free positive matrix, one sweep worked in full; what each kind of matrix does to a signal mixed again and again (doubly stochastic, free, orthogonal); and the set itself, the Birkhoff polytope, with its corners. A single matrix per kind here: actual mHC learns matrices that depend on the input.

ᵘr̲owCondition : Int -> Float

function · line 74

No entry negative, every row and every column summing to 1. Of the n^2 entries, the 2 n sums fix all but (n - 1)^2: the sums are not independent (the rows' total and the columns' total are both n, so one of the 2 n conditions follows from the others), and 2 n - 1 of them fix as many entries. Counted: entries, independent conditions, free entries. The conditions written as a matrix, one row per sum (n row sums, then n column sums), one column per entry (by rows): its rank is the number of independent conditions, which Matrix computes; the free entries are the rest. The formula (n - 1)^2 is printed beside it.

twoByTwo : Float

value · line 164

Of size 2 the doubly stochastic matrices are (p, 1 - p / 1 - p, p), p from 0 to 1: one number, a segment whose two ends are the two permutation matrices (the swap at p = 0, the identity at p = 1). Of size 3 the corners are the six permutation matrices (Birkhoff's theorem: every doubly stochastic matrix is a mix of them), each on the set and orthogonal. At a corner the set is not smooth: from the identity along D = J / 3 - I (rows and columns summing to 0, so the sums stay 1) a step of +0.1 stays in the set and a step of -0.1 does not (an entry goes negative), where from the middle, uniform mixing, along E = P - P' (P a cyclic shift) both directions stay in: the smallest entry at -0.1, 0, +0.1 from each.
