librarylibs/Manifold/src/Manifold.xtl

Manifold: the unit sphere, the Poincare ball, and the pictures of the circle, the sphere, the torus and the Swiss roll: the distance measured along the space, putting a point onto it, the tangent part of a vector at a point, a step from a point along a tangent vector that stays on the space and the arrow that undoes it, a mean that stays on the space, how far off it a point is; the points of each space at given coordinates of its map, for drawing it; and the distortion of an embedding's distances against those it should keep. Import it with an alias of your choice: "mf:" u_se< "Manifold". Put libs/Manifold/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Manifold/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

A point is a vector of Floats, in any number of dimensions; a set of points is a matrix, one point per row. Each space has a name that starts its functions (s_phere, b_all the Poincare ball, c_ircle, t_orus, r_oll the Swiss roll), and the word after it is the same verb for every space: Project (onto it), Distance (along it), Tangent (the part of a vector along it at a point), Step (from a point, along a tangent vector, staying on it), Arrow (the tangent vector a Step would take from one point to another), Mean (of a set, on it), Off (how far a point is from it); and for drawing, Points (the points at rows of coordinates of the space's map), Arc or Geodesic (points along the shortest path between two points). So a program written for the sphere reads the same on the ball. Every result is a Float.
