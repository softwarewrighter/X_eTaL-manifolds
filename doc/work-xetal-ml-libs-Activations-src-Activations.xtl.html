librarywork/xetal-ml/libs/Activations/src/Activations.xtl

Activations: activation functions, a derivative for each, and the gated layers (GLU, ReGLU, GEGLU, SwiGLU)

Import it with an alias of your choice: "act:" u_se< "Activations". Put libs/Activations/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Activations/docs. Names with l: are exported; h: names are private to this file.

Every activation works item by item on a Float array of any shape, so a batch (one example per row) goes through in one call. A setting comes first (0.1 ᵃᶜᵗl̲eaky x, 1.5 ᵃᶜᵗs̲wish x); PReLU's slopes are one per column (the last axis), as a bias is. Each function f has its derivative d_f written in terms of f's input (ᵃᶜᵗd̲gelu z, not of its output): X_eTaL has no automatic differentiation, and the smooth activations cannot be written at their output as ReLU, tanh and sigmoid can. A gated layer takes its two weight arrays as a tuple, (W, V) ᵃᶜᵗs̲wiglu x: the activation on x W, times the linear x V (Shazeer 2020's order).

tanh and erf are X_eTaL's builtins, t̲anh and e̲rf (libm's, at full accuracy: docs/xetal-asks.md M19, landed); ᵃᶜᵗt̲anh is the builtin under this library's name, beside its other activations.
