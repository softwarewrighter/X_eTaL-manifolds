programdemos/mhc-explorer/mhc-depth.xtl

mHC explorer, part 2: from matrices to networks. A stack of small residual layers over n streams (mHC's layer, Mixing's s_tack) with four kinds of mixing made from the same trained numbers: the plain residual (the identity), free mixing (hyper-connections), doubly stochastic mixing (Sinkhorn's iteration on exp, mHC's) and orthogonal mixing (the Cayley transform). First at the start of training, depth by depth: the size of the signal going up, of the gradient coming down, the streams' spread, the composite gain and the constraint's violation, over several seeds. Then trained on the kit's flat clusters at four depths: loss by step, held-out accuracy, whether the run stayed finite, the cost of a step. The full training is done offline by this program (train/train.sh) and its records read back from data/; the page trains the first steps at the smallest depth again, and they must equal the records.
