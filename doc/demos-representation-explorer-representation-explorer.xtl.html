programdemos/representation-explorer/representation-explorer.xtl

Representation explorer, part 1: flat against spherical. The same points kept two ways, as they are (a flat space, the straight line between them) and as their directions on the unit sphere (the angle between them), on two data sets of the kit built with a known structure: flat clusters, whose structure is the straight line's, and directions, whose structure is the angle's. For each: three distances between the same pairs, a shortest path in each space, a mean in each space, nearest neighbors and a decision boundary under each distance, a small classifier trained on each representation (the same network, seeds, split and steps), and how badly each representation keeps the data's own distances. The first test of H1.
