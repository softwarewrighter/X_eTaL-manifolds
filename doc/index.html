  • library libs/Kit/src/Kit.xtl: Kit: the experiment kit's data sets, each with a known structure and made from its own seed: flat (Gaussian clusters), directional (classes that differ by direction, not length) and hierarchical (a tree, its distances along the tree, a class per branch, and a plain layout of it in the plane); and the seeded numbers they are made of. Import it with an alias of your choice: "kt:" u_se< "Kit". Put libs/Kit/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Kit/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.
  • library libs/Manifold/src/Manifold.xtl: Manifold: the unit sphere, the Poincare ball, and the pictures of the circle, the sphere, the torus and the Swiss roll: the distance measured along the space, putting a point onto it, the tangent part of a vector at a point, a step from a point along a tangent vector that stays on the space and the arrow that undoes it, a mean that stays on the space, how far off it a point is; the points of each space at given coordinates of its map, for drawing it; and the distortion of an embedding's distances against those it should keep. Import it with an alias of your choice: "mf:" u_se< "Manifold". Put libs/Manifold/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Manifold/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.
  • library work/xetal-libraries/libs/Trig/src/Trig.xtl: Trig: trigonometric and hyperbolic functions not in the base language, written in closed form from its builtins (s_in, c_os, a_tan, s_qrt, e_xp, e_xpm1, l_og, l_og1p). The hyperbolic tangent is the builtin t_anh; Trig keeps no copy of it. Import it with an alias of your choice: "tr:" u_se< "Trig". Put libs/Trig/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Trig/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.
  • library work/xetal-libraries/libs/Graphs/src/Graphs.xtl: Graphs: graphs as adjacency matrices -- from edges, degrees, reachability, shortest paths, breadth-first levels, connected components. Import it with an alias of your choice: "g:" u_se< "Graphs". Put libs/Graphs/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Graphs/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.
  • macro library work/xetal-libraries/libs/Graphs/src/Graphs.xtlm: Graphs: its macro library -- g_raph<, a graph written by its names. Imported with the functions, under one alias: "g:" u_se< "Graphs". A macro is a function from the source text written left and right of its call to the source that replaces the call, run before the program is compiled (X_eTaL MC10). Names with m: are macros; those under h: are private to this file.
  • system macros std/System.xtlm: The system macros (lang-choices MC18, MC19, MC24), loaded before every file with no import and no alias: each is called unprefixed, as "c" i_f< "a; b". A macro is a function of the source text on each side of its call; the text it gives replaces the call. @ on a side means the macro takes nothing there (MC22). What only the compiler knows comes from hooks (MC20), usable only in a macro body.

  • program demos/manifold-tour/manifold-tour.xtl: Manifold tour: five spaces this repository's experiments live on, the circle, the sphere, the torus, the Swiss roll and the Poincare disk, each drawn from its map (its own coordinates) into the space around it, and on each one measurement of what a manifold is: two points near each other are nearly as far apart along the space as through it (locally flat), two points far apart are not; on the roll, near through the air can be far along the sheet; on the disk, near the rim a small step on paper is a long way. Nothing is trained.
  • program demos/representation-explorer/representation-explorer.xtl: Representation explorer, part 1: flat against spherical. The same points kept two ways, as they are (a flat space, the straight line between them) and as their directions on the unit sphere (the angle between them), on two data sets of the kit built with a known structure: flat clusters, whose structure is the straight line's, and directions, whose structure is the angle's. For each: three distances between the same pairs, a shortest path in each space, a mean in each space, nearest neighbors and a decision boundary under each distance, a small classifier trained on each representation (the same network, seeds, split and steps), and how badly each representation keeps the data's own distances. The first test of H1.
  • library work/xetal-ml/libs/Activations/src/Activations.xtl: Activations: activation functions, a derivative for each, and the gated layers (GLU, ReGLU, GEGLU, SwiGLU)
  • macro library work/xetal-ml/libs/Net/src/Net.xtlm: Net: a network written as one line -- its layer sizes and activations become ordinary NN calls
  • program demos/sphere-hello/sphere-hello.xtl: Sphere hello: a handful of points on the unit sphere. Two ways to measure how far apart two of them are, the straight line through the sphere and the great circle along it, order every point's neighbors the same way; and the plain average of the points is not on the sphere, where the mean along it is.
