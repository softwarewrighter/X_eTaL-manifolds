programdemos/manifold-tour/manifold-tour.xtl

Manifold tour: five spaces this repository's experiments live on, the circle, the sphere, the torus, the Swiss roll and the Poincare disk, each drawn from its map (its own coordinates) into the space around it, and on each one measurement of what a manifold is: two points near each other are nearly as far apart along the space as through it (locally flat), two points far apart are not; on the roll, near through the air can be far along the sheet; on the disk, near the rim a small step on paper is a long way. Nothing is trained.
